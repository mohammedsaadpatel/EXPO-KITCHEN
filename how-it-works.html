How a rush runs on EXPO

Add the order

Type the ticket or table, list the items (one per line or comma separated), pick a target time with a preset. Customer allergies are tagged per item and print as a red warning on the receipt.

Tap items as they finish

Every item is a huge button. The progress bar on the card shows how close the order is.

Watch the board shift

Cards turn calm, warning, late. Colour changes, size grows, motion starts. Late cards double in width and flash.

Send it

When all items are ticked the button pulses green. One tap serves the order and dims it.

Oops? Undo

Every action offers an UNDO toast. Ctrl+Z steps back up to 40 actions.

Urgency states

CALM

Plenty of time. Green, normal size, still.

WARNING

Under 30% of time left or last minute. Amber, larger, pulsing.

LATE

Past target. Red, biggest, shaking, screen edge glows.
